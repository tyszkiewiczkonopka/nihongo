Which particle marks what.

Each particle comes after the word it applies to and marks that word's role in the sentence — object, direction, place, or time.

を o direct object
accusative
Xを verbobject of the action
りんごをたべます。 Eat an apple.
へ e direction of movement
Xへdestination of movement
がっこうへいきます。 Go to school.
に ni destination · location · time
Xにdestination of movement
がっこうにいきます。 Go to school.
Xに います / ありますlocation (existence)
がっこうにいます。 Be at school.
(time)にpoint in time
8じにいきます。 Go at 8:00.
で de place of action
(place)でwhere the action happens
がっこうでたべます。 Eat at school.
と to with (together)
and (joining nouns)
Xとtogether with someone
ともだちといきます。 Go with a friend.
XとYjoins nouns: “and”
ねこといぬ。 A cat and a dog.
